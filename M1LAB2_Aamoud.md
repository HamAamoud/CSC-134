# Robot Sandwich

## Assumptions
- The robot's gripper starts empty.
- The bread, peanut butter, and a knife are on the counter.

## Steps
1. Untwist the twist tie on the plastic bread bag.
2. Open the bag.
3. Take the bread out of the bag.
4. Put the bread down.
5. Open the peanut butter.
6. Remove seal from the peanut butter
7. Pick up the knife.
8. Cut two slices from the loaf of bread.
9. Wash the knife.
10. Dip the knife in the peanut butter
11. Spread the peanut butter on one slice of bread.
12. Set the knife down.
13. Place slice 1 on the plate.
14. Place slice 2 on top of slice 1.
15. Close the peanut butter.

## Done When
The sandwich is ordered bread, peanut butter, bread. The sandwich is on the table.
